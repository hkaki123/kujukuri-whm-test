Kujukuri Town Hazard Map

How to use

○ Browse the hazard map of Kujukuri town.

[Tsunami and Storm Surge Inundation Simulation Area Map]
  • Maximum simulated tsunami (Level 2 tsunami)
    • Simulated range and height of inundation based on the Act on Development of Areas Resilient to Tsunami, at times of maximum-class (Level 2) tsunami under a bad condition.
  • Major Tsunami Warning 10m
    • Simulated range and height of inundation at times of "Major Tsunami Warning 10m (tsunami height approx. 7-10m), based on the "Tsunami Inundation Simulation Map (FY2014-2015 Chiba Prefecture Earthquake Damage Estimation Survey Results)" created by Chiba Prefecture.
  • Otsunami warning 5m
    • Simulated range and height of inundation at times of "Major Tsunami Warning 5m (tsunami height approx. 3-5m), based on the "Tsunami Inundation Simulation Map (FY2014-2015 Chiba Prefecture Earthquake Damage Estimation Survey Results)" created by Chiba Prefecture.
  • Tsunami warning 3m
    • Simulated range and height of inundation at times of "Tsunami Warning ３m (tsunami height approx. 1-3m), based on the "Tsunami Inundation Simulation Map (FY2014-2015 Chiba Prefecture Earthquake Damage Estimation Survey Results)" created by Chiba Prefecture.
  • Storm Surge Inundation
    • Simulated range and depth of inundation in case of flooding caused by the largest possible storm surge (a typhoon on the scale of the Muroto Typhoon), based on the "Storm Surge Inundation Area Map (Chiba East Coast)" created by Chiba Prefecture under the Flood Control Act.
  • Tsunami From Japan Trench Earthquake
    • Simulated range and height of inundation in the Japan Trench (off Sanriku and Hidaka coasts) models examined at the Cabinet Office's Study Group on Modeling of Mega Earthquakes along the Japan Trench and the Kuril Islands Trench(https://www.bousai.go.jp/jishin/nihonkaiko_chishima/model/index.html).
  • Tsunami From Kuril Islands Trench Earthquakes.
    • Simulated range and height of inundation in the Kuril Islands Trench (off Tokachi and Nemuro coasts) models examined at the Cabinet Office's Study Group on Modeling of Mega Earthquakes along the Japan Trench and the Kuril Islands Trench(https://www.bousai.go.jp/jishin/nihonkaiko_chishima/model/index.html).
[Notes on viewing the map]
・The range and height of inundation shown on the map are results of calculated simulations based on arbitrary estimations.
・The range and height of inundation may be significantly different depending on various conditions such as the size of the earthquake, the location of the epicenter, etc.

[Flood Inundation Simulation Area Map]
  • Maximum Simulation
    • Simulated range and height of inundation at times of flood in rivers due to the maximum simulated rainfall (heavy rainfall of approx. once in 1,000 years).
    Based on the "Flood Inundation Simulation Area Map (Maximum Simulation)" published by Chiba Prefecture, the map is made from layers of areas of the five rivers (Magame, Sakuda, Nabaki, Kido, and Horikawa Rivers) that may affect the town.

    [Simulated rainfall]
    ・ Magame River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Magame River basin - 690.0mm
    ・ Sakuda River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Sakuda River basin - 686.5mm
    ・ Nabaki River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Nabaki River basin - 663.7mm
    ・ Kido River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Kido River basin - 676.5mm
    ・ Horikawa River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Horikawa River basin - 690.0mm

    ・ Assumed Area of Building Collapse and Flood (Bank Erosion)
    Areas with risks of bank erosions that may wash away or collapse the houses at times of flood due to maximum simulated rainfall.

  • Planned-scale
    • Simulated range and height of inundation at times of river flood due to planned-scale rainfall (heavy rainfall of approx. once in 50 years).
    Based on the "Flood Inundation Simulation Area Map (Planned Scale)" published by Chiba Prefecture, the map is made from layers of areas of the three rivers (Magame, Sakuda, and Nabaki Rivers) that may affect the town.

    [Assumed rainfall]
    ・Magame River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Magame River basin - 288.0mm
    ・Sakuda River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Sakuda River basin - 287.0mm
    ・Nabaki River: Total rainfall in 24 hrs. in the Nabaki River basin - 288.3mm
[Notes on viewing the map]
・The range and height of inundation shown on the map are results of calculated simulations based on arbitrary estimations.
・The range and height of inundation may be significantly different depending on various conditions such as the rainfall, etc.
・Floods from storm surge and inland flood are not taken into consideration.
